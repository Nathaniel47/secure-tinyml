Browser demo: fp32 vs int8 vs defended

Everything runs in your browser (ONNX Runtime Web, WASM): your image never leaves the page. Upload or photograph something, optionally paste an adversarial patch (trained to say airplane against the fp32 model) and see how the three models react. The defended model is the int8 model fine-tuned with adversarial training.

The models know 10 CIFAR-10 classes at 32x32 pixels, so your picture is cropped to a square and shrunk first; expect poor accuracy on ordinary photos. Research demo, not a security product.
